# Character Advancement

Over the course of a {{campaign}}, {{player characters}} are expected
to grow and develop in knowledge, depth, and power. A substantial part of this
growth occurs through advancement of the {{campaign}}'s story, such as when
the {{pcs}} discovery a secret or develop a relationship with an
{{npc}}. Additionally, the advancement of a {{pc}}'s potential is
represented by the advancement of their {{skill}} {{levels}}.

Advancement can occur in a number of ways during a {{campaign}}. The most
common reasons that a {{skill}} advances is due to the following, all of
which are described in the sections below:
* Using the Learn and Teach {{abilities}} of the
  [Reason](skills:mundane:reason) and [Scholarship](skills:mundane:scholarship)
  {{skills}}.
* Using the Study {{ability}} of the
  [Investigation](skills:mundane:investigation) {{skill}}, the Train
  {{ability}} of the [Tactics](skills:mundane:tactics) {{skill}}, or
  the Practice {{ability}} of the [Engineering](skills:mundane:engineering)
  {{skill}}.
* Spending an {{exceptional_point}} to advance a {{path_skill}} or an
  {{domain_skill}} during {{ellipsis}}.
* An award by the {{narrator}} due to events of the story.
